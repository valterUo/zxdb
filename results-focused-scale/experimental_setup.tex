\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Workloads and input generation}} \\
Rule workloads & Identity, local complementation, spider fusion, pivot, gadget fusion, bialgebra, boundary pivot and gadget pivot. Each benchmark applies the named simplifier to convergence. \\
Rule size grid & $N\in\{32,64,128,256,512,1024,2048\}$ disconnected motif copies. $N$ is not the vertex count. \\
Instances per rule/size & 10 structurally different input graphs; 70 planned graphs per rule. \\
Motif variation & Mix size-two and size-three motifs of the same rule. Sample ten distinct larger-motif counts without replacement from $\{0,\ldots,\max(9,\lfloor N/4\rfloor)\}$. Remaining copies use size-two motifs. \\
Reproducibility & Mixture sampling uses a deterministic seed derived from family and size; instance seeds 0--9 shuffle component insertion order. The actual mixture counts and input fingerprints are saved. Different mixture counts produce different vertex counts within a rule/size cell. \\
Full-reduction workload & Random Clifford+T circuits, eight qubits, with no planted rewrite motifs. \\
Circuit size grid & Generator depths $d\in\{32,64,128,256,512\}$; these are generator parameters, not physical circuit depths after scheduling. \\
Circuit generation & PyZX CNOT/Hadamard/phase generator, $p_{\mathrm{had}}=0.20$, $p_t=0.25$, \texttt{clifford=False}; seeds 100--109 at each depth. \\
Planned campaign size & 560 rule inputs plus 50 circuit inputs: 610 cases in total. \\
Applicability check & Each active motif size is checked for a target-rule application using PyZX, outside timed trials. \\
Input safety caps & 2,000,000 vertices and 4,000,000 edges, checked after construction; these are not memory limits or output-size caps. \\

\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Execution and resource limits}} \\
ZXdb variant & \texttt{optimized}: Cypher implementation with independent interior-pivot/local-complementation batches and exclusive-access scan reductions. Incremental worklists and the C++ query-module variant are not selected. \\
Database access & Dedicated local Memgraph via Bolt; graph-scoped loading and cleanup. No competing writers are permitted by the optimized execution contract. \\
PyZX execution & Fresh isolated subprocess for each attempt, operating on a copy of the same original input used by ZXdb. \\
Warmups / repetitions & One excluded warmup and two measured repetitions per input and engine: 20 planned measured pairs per ten-input cell. \\
Timing boundary & Reduction to convergence only. Generation, graph copying/loading, output reading, process startup/IPC and validation are outside the reported reduction time. \\
Execution order & Ascending sizes within workload series, interleaved series, shuffled instance order within a size; order seed 20260908. Engine order alternates across trials with a seeded initial offset. Added families follow the preserved original campaign order. \\
Database attempt budget & 15 s for loading, reduction and reading, enforced through query deadlines; individual queries have a 10 s cap. \\
PyZX attempt budget & 15 s watchdog budget covering imports, input reading, copying, reduction and output serialization after watchdog startup; outer subprocess wall timeout is 25 s. \\
Whole-case budget & 120 worker seconds across retries, including setup and validation. Database recovery has a separate allowance. \\
PyZX memory threshold & Sampled RSS threshold of 4096 MiB, checked every 50 ms; excludes Memgraph and the parent process. This is not a hard OS memory cap. \\
Thread settings & OMP, OpenBLAS, MKL and NumExpr thread-count environment variables set to one. These do not constrain Memgraph CPU allocation. \\
Adaptive stopping & Stop a workload series after three consecutive cases where both engines time out. A timeout suppresses further attempts by that engine within the case. Whole-case budget exhaustion separately blocks subsequent cases in the series. \\
Recovery / resume & Automatic restart of the dedicated \texttt{memgraph} container; at most one retry per case across sessions, subject to the cumulative case budget. Completed measured pairs are retained. \\

\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Aggregation and correctness evidence}} \\
Per-size timing curves & Median of per-input median reduction times. Main paired curves require complete cells; separate capacity curves show available successful engine timings. \\
Paired speedup & PyZX reduction time divided by ZXdb reduction time. Compute the median log ratio within each distinct-input group, then average across groups and exponentiate. \\
Across-size aggregation & Equal weight for each represented size, then equal weight for distinct input groups within each size. No pooling across different rules. \\
Intervals & 95\% hierarchical bootstrap intervals, 2000 resamples. Resample input groups and paired repetitions within each size; sizes remain fixed. Bootstrap seed 17 for per-size intervals and 20260910 for across-size intervals. \\
Primary summary range & 512, 1024 and 2048 copies for the five initially favorable rules: 30 planned inputs and 60 measured pairs per rule. \\
Additional table ranges & Bialgebra, boundary pivot and gadget pivot: full 32--2048-copy grid. Full reduction: depth 32--512. Gadget fusion additionally has an explicitly exploratory 1024--2048-copy selection. \\
Missing-data policy & Displayed conditional summaries use successful measured pairs only and disclose contributing sizes and coverage. Their intervals do not correct selection bias. Strict full-grid estimates remain separately unavailable for incomplete grids. \\
Input grouping & Exact saved input fingerprints identify duplicate inputs; these fingerprints are not canonical graph-isomorphism certificates. \\
Tensor validation & Compare ordered tensors up to nonzero global scalar, with explicit zero handling. Limited to 32 vertices and 20 edges plus open legs; residual threshold $10^{-8}$. Larger checks are inconclusive. \\
Structural comparison & Output graph isomorphism checks preserve types, phases, wire types and boundary roles. Exact search is capped at 256 vertices, 4096 edges and a cooperative 1 s deadline. Degree histograms are also compared. Structural agreement alone is not semantic verification. \\

\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Recorded software and host}} \\
Software & Python 3.12.10; PyZX 0.10.3; Memgraph 3.6.2; Neo4j Python driver 5.28.1; NumPy 2.2.5; psutil 7.0.0. \\
Database storage & \texttt{IN\_MEMORY\_ANALYTICAL}; reported allocation limit 23.47 GiB in all recorded server snapshots. This is not a measurement of host RAM or a Docker memory limit. \\
Recorded indexes & Node properties \texttt{graph\_id}, \texttt{id}, \texttt{t}; Wire properties \texttt{id}, \texttt{t}; pivot/lcomp worklist labels are indexed but incremental worklists are not enabled. \\
Host & Windows 11, build 26200; AMD64 Family 25 Model 80 Stepping 0; 16 logical CPUs reported. \\
Unrecorded hardware details & Exact CPU product name, physical RAM, Docker CPU/RAM allocation and background load were not recorded. They should not be inferred from the logical CPU count or Memgraph allocation limit. \\
Provenance & Saved input payloads, plan history, package versions, source hashes, session records and raw per-case JSONL observations. The extension retains earlier focused-campaign checkpoints; no older adaptive-campaign data is imported. \\
